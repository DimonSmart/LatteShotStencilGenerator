# ENG-0001.rule-browser-csharp-runtime

## Rule

Implement the MVP as a .NET 10 Blazor WebAssembly application, and keep the
business logic and geometry engine in C#.

## Applicability

Always

## Rationale

The accepted target runtime is .NET 10 with Blazor WebAssembly, while the
product requires fully local browser processing and deterministic geometry.

## Guidance

Keep JavaScript limited to browser-specific integration such as downloads and
3D viewing. Do not move business or geometry behavior into JavaScript.

## Verification

The application runs as a Blazor WebAssembly client, and business and geometry
implementation is present in C# rather than JavaScript.
