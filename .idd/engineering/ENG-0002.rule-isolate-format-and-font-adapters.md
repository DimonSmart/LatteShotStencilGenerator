# ENG-0002.rule-isolate-format-and-font-adapters

## Rule

Isolate file-format and font dependencies behind project-owned adapters. This
includes STL import/export, 3MF import/export, SVG parsing, and font-outline
generation.

## Applicability

Conditional

## Applies when

- adding or changing STL, 3MF, SVG, font, or serialization integration

## Rationale

The selected browser libraries solve different concerns and may evolve at
different speeds. The stencil domain must not become coupled to Three.js scene
objects, Manifold binding objects, SVG-loader objects, OpenType objects, or a
specific 3MF writer.

## Guidance

- Convert external library data into project-owned template mesh/solid,
  planar-artwork, glyph-outline, material-region, and export-model contracts at
  adapter boundaries.
- Keep rendering types out of geometry/domain state.
- Keep 3MF color/material semantics in an export adapter rather than encoding
  slicer-specific details throughout the application.
- Make format adapters replaceable without changing bridge or placement logic.

## Verification

Core stencil logic consumes project-owned geometry contracts, and each external
format/font library can be substituted without changing the product behavior
contract.
