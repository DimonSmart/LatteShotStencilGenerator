# ENG-0003.rule-specialized-polygon-operations

## Rule

Use Manifold (manifold-3d WASM) as the authoritative geometry kernel for solid
boolean operations and, where practical, its CrossSection facilities for planar
boolean/topology work. Do not implement triangle-mesh CSG or polygon boolean
algorithms locally.

Three.js is a rendering and format-integration layer, not the boolean geometry
kernel.

## Applicability

Conditional

## Applies when

- implementing template solid import normalization, artwork extrusion,
  subtraction, union, bridge geometry, connectivity checks, or other 2D/3D
  boolean operations

## Rationale

The product now modifies user-provided 3D solids rather than generating one
known plate from scratch. Robust topology is more important than minimizing
library count. Manifold is specifically designed for topologically robust solid
modeling and provides browser WASM bindings.

## Guidance

- Normalize imported meshes into indexed finite geometry before creating a
  Manifold solid.
- Perform only conservative welding/normalization needed for valid input.
  Reject meshes that cannot become a valid printable solid rather than adding a
  home-grown repair subsystem.
- Build the SVG cutter and bridges as precise planar/cross-section geometry and
  extrude them through the template before boolean operations.
- Dispose temporary Manifold objects promptly during interactive editing.
- Do not use Three.js mesh modifiers as a second independent source of geometry
  truth.

## Verification

Representative STL/3MF templates survive artwork subtraction and bridge union
as valid solids; malformed inputs fail explicitly; geometry results do not
depend on custom ad-hoc CSG code.
