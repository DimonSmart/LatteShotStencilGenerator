# IDD-0006.spec-2d-preview-and-stl-export

## Intent

The workspace preview faithfully communicates the final normalized 2D stencil
geometry, while mesh construction and validation are deferred until STL export.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0002
- IDD-0003
- IDD-0004
- IDD-0005

## Behavior

- The 2D preview shows card and working-area boundaries, stencil openings,
  bridges, caption geometry, inversion results, and spatially relevant errors
  and warnings.
- The 2D preview is derived directly from the current normalized
  `CardGeometry`; triangulation is not part of preview construction.
- Geometry validation occurs while building `CardGeometry`. A geometry error
  is shown to the user, blocks download, and is cleared after a successful
  rebuild.
- Manufacturability findings remain advisory warnings and do not block export
  when `CardGeometry.IsExportable` is true.
- Final geometry is triangulated and extruded into lower and multi-level upper
  surfaces with vertical outer and inner walls in one mesh only when STL
  download is requested.
- The generated mesh is validated before serialization. The same mesh instance
  is used for validation and binary STL serialization.
- Export produces binary STL in millimetre convention. The lower surface is
  normalized to Z=0; XY may be normalized to (0,0) for stability.
- The downloaded name uses uppercase caption when present, otherwise the source
  filename plus `-card.stl`.
- A mesh-generation or mesh-validation failure reports an export error without
  resetting the imported artwork, placement, bridges, caption, or current 2D
  geometry.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- `CardGeometry` is the durable workspace representation used by the preview.
- `CardMeshGenerator.Generate` and `MeshValidator.Validate` are export-only
  operations in the UI flow.
- `MaximumFlattenedSvgSegments` is a 2D import/flattening limit.
- `MaximumMeshTriangles` is an STL mesh limit and does not block import,
  placement, bridge editing, caption editing, or 2D preview.
- Mesh validation checks non-emptiness, watertightness, manifoldness, valid
  coordinates, and expected bounding box within tolerance before download.

## Non-goals

- Binary identity or triangle ordering parity with a reference STL is not
  required.
- Additional mesh visualization is outside the current workspace architecture.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Workspace editing updates the 2D preview without invoking
  `CardMeshGenerator.Generate` or `MeshValidator.Validate`.
- A downloadable STL is built from the current `CardGeometry`, validated, and
  serialized from one generated mesh.
- Mesh-specific failures appear only when export is attempted and leave the
  workspace intact.
- A downloadable STL is watertight, manifold, and opens in OrcaSlicer and in
  PrusaSlicer or Cura.

## Verification

- Verify the 2D preview directly reflects `CardGeometry` for openings, nested
  holes, islands, bridges, caption, and inversion.
- Verify geometry refresh has no mesh-generation path.
- Verify extrusion, STL output, watertight/manifold properties, expected bounds
  and Z levels, and absence of invalid triangles.
