# IDD-0005.spec-stencil-topology-and-manufacturability

## Intent

Cutting artwork must not turn enclosed stencil details into loose pieces.
Automatic bridge generation keeps every retained artwork island physically
connected to the stencil while preserving as much of the artwork as practical.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0002
- IDD-0003
- IDD-0006

## Behavior

- After applying the artwork cut conceptually, the application identifies
  retained material that would become disconnected from the main template.
  Typical examples are centres of O, A, P, R, 0, 8, and enclosed logo details.
- Automatic bridge generation is enabled by default and deterministically
  connects every detached retained island to connected template material.
- Bridge generation repeats with a topology/connectivity recheck until no
  detached printable components remain or the application can explain why a
  valid bridge cannot be constructed.
- A bridge returns a narrow material strip across an artwork opening. It remains
  inside the configured artwork area and does not extend outside the template.
- Bridge width is configurable in millimetres and is never below the configured
  minimum. The reference/default minimum is 0.8 mm.
- Automatic mode is the normal workflow. A bridge-off mode may be exposed for
  artwork that naturally has no islands, but export is blocked when disabling
  bridges would leave detached components.
- The application warns about very thin walls, tiny retained features, tiny
  openings, or bridge widths that are likely to be difficult to print.
  Manufacturability warnings are visible but do not silently reshape artwork.
- The final boolean result is checked for non-empty, printable solid geometry
  before export.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- Island detection is defined by physical connectivity of the resulting
  stencil, not merely by SVG contour nesting.
- Bridge decisions are made from normalized planar artwork/template geometry and
  validated against the resulting 3D solid.
- The same bridge geometry is used by preview, STL export, and 3MF export.

## Non-goals

- Aesthetic matching of bridge positions from the historical Donut model and
  manual bridge drawing are not initial requirements.
- Full mesh healing for broken uploaded templates is not part of bridge logic.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Artwork containing enclosed regions exports as one physically connected
  stencil after automatic bridging.
- Bridges stay inside the artwork area, meet the configured minimum width, and
  do not create non-manifold output.
- Disabling bridges while islands remain blocks export with a clear explanation.
- Identical inputs produce identical bridge positions.

## Verification

- Test representative letters, digits, logos, nested holes, multiple islands,
  curved contours, and no-island artwork.
- Verify 3D connectedness after the final template-minus-artwork-plus-bridges
  operation, not only the 2D preview.
