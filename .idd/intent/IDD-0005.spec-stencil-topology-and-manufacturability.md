# IDD-0005.spec-stencil-topology-and-manufacturability

## Intent

The generated card is a single printable stencil: artwork openings must not
leave detached material islands, and manufacturability issues must be visible to
the user.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0002
- IDD-0003
- IDD-0006

## Behavior

- After subtracting artwork, the app identifies material components disconnected
  from the card's outer material. Typical islands include centres of O, A, P,
  R, and enclosed logo details.
- Automatic bridge generation deterministically joins every material island to
  connected material, repeating until no detached components remain. Bridge
  geometry is incorporated into the same normalised planar geometry as artwork
  before topology is rechecked. Bridges return a narrow material region to an
  opening and remain in the working area.
- In MVP bridge placement is automatic. `BridgeWidthMm` and `BridgeMode`
  (`Auto` or `Off`) are exposed settings. Off is allowed only when no islands
  remain; otherwise export is warned or blocked.
- Bridge geometry can be rectangular or capsule-like, and its width is never
  below the configured minimum.
- The app warns about walls, openings, bridges, small components, or SVG details
  violating configured minimum wall thickness, feature size, or generation
  resolution. It does not silently and aggressively alter artistic geometry.
- The Reference / Donut configuration defaults its minimum wall thickness and
  minimum feature size to 0.8 mm, and its generation-resolution threshold to
  0.2 mm. These values are configurable.
- Manufacturability findings are non-blocking warnings: users may export a
  stencil that falls below a configured threshold.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- The working zone, base plate, outer raised area, artwork openings, bridges,
  and embossed caption form one watertight model rather than separate inserts.
- Bridge settings support future automatic-algorithm configuration such as
  minimum/maximum bridges per island, direction strategy, spacing, corner
  clearance, end shape, and candidate priority; manual bridge editing is not
  required.

## Non-goals

- Aesthetic parity with `Donut.stl` bridge positions and manual bridge placement
  are not MVP requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Generated cards contain no detached retained-material components after bridge
  application and topology recheck.
- Bridges stay inside the working area, meet configured width, and preserve a
  manifold STL.
- The default manufacturability thresholds are visible and configurable, and a
  threshold violation warns without preventing STL export.

## Verification

- Verify island detection and deterministic bridge generation for representative
  letter and logo geometries, including curved imported artwork and the no-island
  `Off` case.
- Verify manufacturing warnings without automatic destructive artwork changes.
- Verify the Reference / Donut default thresholds and that warnings do not
  block export.
