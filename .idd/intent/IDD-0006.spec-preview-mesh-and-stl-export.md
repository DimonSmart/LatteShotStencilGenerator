# IDD-0006.spec-preview-mesh-and-stl-export

## Intent

The 2D and 3D views faithfully communicate the exportable model, and the app
exports a valid binary STL only after critical geometry and mesh validation.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0002
- IDD-0003
- IDD-0004
- IDD-0005

## Behavior

- The 2D preview shows card and working-area boundaries, artwork area,
  openings, bridges, caption, and spatially relevant errors and warnings. It is
  derived from the final normalised 2D geometry used to make the mesh.
- The interactive 3D preview supports rotate, zoom, pan, and camera reset, and
  displays the generated mesh or an equivalent representation from the same
  geometry.
- Final geometry is triangulated and extruded into lower and multi-level upper
  surfaces with vertical outer and inner walls in one mesh.
- The mesh has no open boundaries, non-manifold edges, zero-area triangles,
  invalid coordinates, generator-created self-intersections, inconsistent
  winding, or invalid normals.
- Export produces binary STL in millimetre convention. The lower surface is
  normalised to Z=0; XY may be normalised to (0,0) for stability.
- The downloaded name uses uppercase caption when present, otherwise the source
  filename plus `-card.stl` (for example `donut.svg` becomes `DONUT.stl`, or
  `donut-card.stl` when caption is empty).
- Import, geometry, and mesh errors block export; non-critical warnings may not
  block a valid model.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- Mesh validation checks non-emptiness, watertightness, manifoldness, valid
  coordinates, and expected bounding box within tolerance before download.

## Non-goals

- Binary identity or triangle ordering parity with the reference STL is not
  required.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The previews do not represent geometry that differs from exported STL.
- A downloadable STL is watertight, manifold, and opens in OrcaSlicer and in
  PrusaSlicer or Cura.

## Verification

- Verify extrusion, STL output, watertight/manifold properties, expected bounds
  and Z levels, and absence of invalid triangles.
