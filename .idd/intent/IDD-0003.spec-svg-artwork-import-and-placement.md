# IDD-0003.spec-svg-artwork-import-and-placement

## Intent

The MVP imports a safe, explicit subset of fill-based SVG artwork and converts
it into stencil openings that fit within the working area.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001
- IDD-0002
- IDD-0005
- IDD-0006

## Behavior

- Supported geometry is `svg`, `g`, `path`, `rect`, `circle`, `ellipse`,
  `polygon`, closed `polyline`, `viewBox`, nested translate/scale/rotate/matrix
  transforms, and nonzero/evenodd fill rules. Transforms are flattened before
  geometry processing.
- Filled SVG regions become stencil openings by default; `Invert stencil`
  reverses that interpretation.
- Stroke-only artwork is not converted. The app reports an actionable message
  suggesting conversion of strokes to filled paths.
- Unsupported elements that could affect the stencil produce a warning or error
  identifying them. Text, images, raster content, filters, masks, clip paths,
  patterns, gradients as geometry, external CSS/references, scripts,
  animations, and stroke-only artwork are outside the MVP subset.
- Parsing treats SVG as data: scripts, DTD/external entities, external URLs,
  and external-resource loads are prohibited.
- Imported contours are normalised, topologically cleaned, bounded, and fit to
  the artwork area using centred aspect-ratio-preserving `Contain` placement.
- Users can adjust scale percentage and X/Y offsets or reset placement. Artwork
  outside the working area is a validation error that blocks export until
  corrected or refitted.
- Limits are explicit: SVG files up to 5 MB and up to 5,000 source shape/path
  elements; flattened-segment and final-mesh limits are configurable. Exceeding
  a limit yields a clear error rather than a hung tab.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- Import validation checks file presence and size, valid XML, usable closed
  filled contours, and absence of unsafe external dependencies.

## Non-goals

- Best-effort rendering of unsupported SVG and automatic stroke-to-outline
  conversion are not required.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Valid supported SVGs automatically fit without distorted proportions.
- Unsupported or unsafe input is explained rather than silently changing the
  stencil.
- Artwork cannot be exported outside the working area.

## Verification

- Maintain a corpus covering circle, rectangle, ring, O, A, nested holes,
  evenodd, transformed path, multiple islands, invalid stroke-only, and
  unsupported-element cases; verify transforms, fill rules, bounds, cleanup,
  and fit properties.
