# IDD-0002.spec-reference-donut-card

## Intent

The built-in, read-only `Reference / Donut` preset defines the default card
layout and reference geometry for the MVP. Its dimensions use millimetres.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001
- IDD-0004
- IDD-0005
- IDD-0006

## Behavior

- Card-local 2D coordinates start at the top-left card corner; X increases
  rightward and Y downward. Z=0 is the model bottom. Source STL absolute
  coordinates do not affect generation.
- The preset card is 88.345 mm wide and 113.882 mm high, with 0.998 mm base
  thickness and a 0.200 mm raised outer/header layer.
- The square working area begins at X=6.678 mm, Y=33.029 mm and measures
  74.989 by 74.989 mm. It remains at base thickness while the surrounding area
  is raised.
- Artwork has 3.0 mm padding and is contained, aspect-ratio preserved, and
  centred in its available area.
- The caption area begins at X=6.678 mm, Y=0 and measures 74.989 by 33.029 mm.
- The preset is never edited. A user change creates a `Custom` working copy.
- All preset dimensions and manufacturing, bridge, and geometry settings are
  configuration available to the geometry engine independently of the UI.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- The reference layout is based on `Donut.stl` coordinates interpreted as
  millimetres, the convention appropriate for 3D printing.

## Non-goals

- Matching the reference STL triangle-for-triangle or recovering its CAD model
  is not required.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The default card bounding box is 88.345 by 113.882 mm within 0.05 mm.
- Working-area size and origin are 74.989 by 74.989 mm and X=6.678, Y=33.029 mm
  within 0.05 mm.
- Default Z levels are approximately 0.998 mm at the base and 1.198 mm on the
  outer/header area.

## Verification

- Golden/reference checks establish default bounding box, working-area
  placement, and Z-level invariants with a 0.05 mm layout tolerance.
